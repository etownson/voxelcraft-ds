# MultiVoxelVerse Adventures  
  
You spawn into a new world. Your initial spawn is a scene highlighted with graphics and sound to designate the importance of the event.   
  
A chat bubble appears that is a transmission from a distant place. All transmissions are understandable but a bit spotty in places. The following comes through:  
- The transmitter tells you that you have been teleported here to fully reactivate the beacons previously placed in the world  
- The transmitter knows you are disoriented from the teleportation process.  
- The transmitter tells you that you need to find and activate a beacon that should be nearby.  
- The transmitter tells you that your mission is urgent but not enough of the transmission comes through to understand why.   
  
You start with no items in your inventory. You can punch trees and gather logs like Minecraft but no crafting has been unlocked because it is gated by the requirement to find recipes. You receive new recipes by finding and activating beacons. Activating a beacon requires essence. You start with just enough essence to do one beacon activation. The recipes you have received are available in a journal. You do not have to do anything further to unlock the recipes. Once you receive them are then ready to be used.  
  
The initial point where you spawn into the world is spawn point 0 (SP0). The first beacon is near SP0 but is just far enough away to require a short bit of exploration.  
  
Enemy difficulty scales based on their distance along the X and Z axes from where they initially spawn versus SP0. Enemies only start additionally scaling in difficulty along the Y axis once they are below sea level. Enemy scaling dimensions are spawn frequency, health, and attack damage.  
  
You earn essence by killing enemies. The amount of essence received scales with the enemy difficulty. Enemies drop essence bubbles within a close random distance near the area where they die. You must pick them up by running over them (intersecting with them). Otherwise they fade out and are lost. They take a short time to appear so that the player has to be more conscious about navigating to pick them while in the heat of battle.  
  
The amount of essence required to unlock a new beacon scales with the beacon’s distance from SP0.  
  
Each time you activate a new beacon you receive a unique transmission with the following:  
- A little more of the story narrative.  
- Some new recipes that help you survive well enough in the outer areas necessary to reach the next beacon.  
- The XYZ coordinate of the next beacon to find. Your current XYZ coordinate is always displayed in the HUD as well as the XYZ coordinate of the next beacon.  Note: The current game’s world generation will need to be adjusted so that the first two beacon positions are created after the initial part of the world is generated such that they make sense. For example, the beacon position cannot be within a body of water.

## Beacon Number 1 Transmission  
You understand the following from the transmission:  
- You are being given recipes to help you survive.   
- You are now incarnated as a biological creature and the transmitter apologizes for subjecting you to the horrors of dealing with health and hunger.  
- The transmitter warns about awful creatures at night but urges you to be brave and extract essence from them.  
- The transmitter expresses urgency for you to find the second beacon but warns you to first establish a base before attempting to activate it.  
  
The initial transmission provides enough recipes so that a player can survive in the area around SP0, establish a base, and ultimately make it to the second beacon for further unlocks.
